What each tracker follows

Seven lobbies whose money to each campaign the site follows: on every profile's Summary of Major Lookouts, the Trackers page, Career Totals and the seating charts. They're shown only; no grade uses them. Below are the exact lists in the code (public/trackers.js and public/industries.js) as of November 14, 2026.

The five kinds of data

A tracker's ✕ ("took some") comes only from money the campaign itself took. The rest is shown beside it.

All seven at a glance

Each tracker

"Took some this cycle" counts sitting members of Congress with at least one check, from the FEC bulk files read on your computer (537 members read, checks through October 14, 2026). "Career" counts members with a check from the tracker's PACs in any cycle since 1979–80.

A PAC matches a tracker by its FEC committee ID (each checked by hand against the committee records on file) or by its name as the FEC writes it. No committee counts for two trackers by ID. Andreessen Horowitz counts for both Crypto and Big Tech + AI; Palantir for both Big Defense and Big Tech + AI.
