Register to Vote Online

Using West Virginia's simple and secure Online Voter Registration system, citizens with a driver's license (or other ID issued by the DMV) can register to vote online.

GoVoteWV

Visit GoVoteWV for absentee voting information, voter registration, County Clerk directory, voter identification information, absentee and provisional ballot tracking, and more.

Campaign Finance Reports

West Virginia has a new, easy-to-use Campaign Finance Reporting System. Candidates and PACs may sign into their campaign finance account, and voters may view campaign finance reports.
