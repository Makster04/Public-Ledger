about us

ADA was founded by Eleanor Roosevelt, John Kenneth Galbraith, Walter Reuther, Arthur Schlesinger, and Reinhold Niebuhr shortly after FDR died.  Our goal then?  To keep the New Deal dream of a fair and prosperous America for all alive for generations to come.

our issues

take action with ADA NOW!

ADA is a premier multi-issue  organization.  But the issues on which we work do not come from the top down.  They come from you.  Every ADA member has a voice.

 

Follow the issue links tolerant more about the issues and to TAKE ACTION with fellow ADA activists!

all issues

Democracy

ADA members fight to expand access to the ballot, register voters, pass the JLVRAA and much more.

Learn More

Economic justice

ADA works alongside labor to ensure workers have dignity in the workplace and to increase wages and benefits.

Learn More

Social Justice

ADA works towards Medicare For All, education and environmental justice and numerous civil rights a racial equity issues.

Elections

Each cycle ADA members tirelessly work to turn out the vote in local, state and national elections. Get involved in a local GOTV campaign today!
