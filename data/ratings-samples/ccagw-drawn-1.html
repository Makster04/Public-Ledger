2025 Congressional Ratings

Since 1989, the Council for Citizens Against Government Waste (CCAGW) has examined roll call votes to help identify which members of Congress have defended taxpayer interests and which have backed away from their promises of fiscal responsibility. The Congressional Ratings separate the praiseworthy from the profligate by evaluating important tax, spending, transparency, and accountability measures. CCAGW applauds those members of Congress who stand up for taxpayers and ignore the temptation of satisfying local and special interests. However, those who support a big-government agenda should be prepared to face the consequences of their spendthrift behavior.

CCAGW’s 2025 Congressional Ratings, for the first session of the 119th Congress, score 25 votes in the House of Representatives and 31 in the Senate. By comparison, CCAGW rated 36 votes in the House of Representatives and eight in the Senate for the second session of the 118th Congress.

CCAGW rates members on a 0-100 percent scale. Members are placed in the following categories: 0-19 percent, Hostile; 20-39 percent, Unfriendly; 40-59 percent, Lukewarm; 60-79 percent, Friendly; 80-99 percent, Taxpayer Hero; and 100 percent, Taxpayer Super Hero.

House and Senate Breakdown

For 2025, four senators and 38 representatives earned the coveted title of Taxpayer Super Hero by achieving the highest possible score of 100 percent. In 2024, one senator and one representative received a perfect score.

For 2025, there were 40 Taxpayer Heroes in the Senate and 102 in the House of Representatives. For 2024, there were 21 Taxpayer Heroes in the Senate and 129 in the House of Representatives.

At the other end of the spectrum, 42 senators had the lowest score of zero percent, and 173 representatives had the lowest score of zero percent. In 2024, 44 senators had the lowest score of zero percent, and 20 representatives had the lowest score of zero percent.

Further Analysis

CCAGW also analyzed ratings based on party affiliation.

The averages were: Senate Republicans – 85 percent, up 9 percentage points from 76 percent in 2024; Senate Democrats, including Independents – 0 percent, down 5 percentage points from 5 percent in 2024. House Republicans – 83 percent, up 3 percentage points from 80 percent in 2024; House Democrats – 2 percent, down 2 percentage points from 4 percent in 2024.

CCAGW congratulates the members who stood by taxpayers and championed fiscal responsibility throughout the first session of the 119th Congress and encourages the constituents of non-Taxpayer Heroes to demand better results from their elected officials.
