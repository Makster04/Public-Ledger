Senate - 119th 2nd Session Vote Descriptions

1. Cloture on the Pregnant Students' Rights Act

Sponsored by Sen. Ashley Moody (R-FL), the Pregnant Students’ Rights Act (S. 3627) would have amended the Higher Education Act to require colleges and universities to inform female students about their rights and protections to carry their pregnancies to term. Under the bill, these women would be informed of available resources and accommodations, such as the opportunity to be granted modified class schedules and excused absences, programs available on campus, and organizations in the local community offering support to young mothers. This bill would help ensure that young women are adequately informed of their options to choose life and continue their education.

This cloture vote was on the Motion to Proceed to the underlying bill, which required 60 votes.

(Failed on 1/27/2026, 47 yeas to 45 nays, Roll Call No. 12)

FRC Action supported this Motion.

2. Motion to Proceed on the Joint Resolution to Reinstate the Biden Administration's VA Abortion Rule

Sponsored by Sen. Richard Blumenthal (D-CT), S. J. Res. 103 would have effectively reinstated the Biden administration’s unlawful rule allowing the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) to provide and counsel in favor of abortion. That rule was in contravention of Section 106 of the Veterans Health Care Act of 1992, which specifically excludes abortions from the list of healthcare services that the VA can provide. In addition to violating federal law, this rule violated state sovereignty by bypassing pro-life state laws, threatened the conscience rights of VA doctors, and increased the number of abortions performed by the VA. The Trump administration’s rule—which undid the Biden administration’s changes and realigned VA policy with federal law by reestablishing the exclusion of abortion and abortion counseling at the VA—should be maintained.

This vote was on the Motion to Proceed to the underlying bill.

(Failed on 3/25/2026, 48 yeas to 50 nays, Roll Call No. 72)

FRC Action opposed this Amendment.

3. Cloture on an Amendment to Protect Women and Girls in Athletics

Sponsored by Sen. Tommy Tuberville (R-AL), S. Amdt. 4421 to the SAVE America Act (S. 1383) mirrors his bill, the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025 (S. 9), and would have restored Title IX’s even playing field for female athletes by making it a violation of federal law for schools that receive federal funds to permit males to participate in an athletic program or activity designated for women or girls.

This cloture vote required 60 votes.

(Failed on 3/21/2026, 49 yeas to 41 nays, Roll Call No. 60)

FRC Action supported this Amendment.

View the Final Vote Results

4. Motion on an Amendment to Extend the Prohibition on Medicaid Funding for Abortion Providers

Sponsored by Sen. Josh Hawley (R-MO), S. Amdt. 4794 to S. Con. Res. 33, the FY 2026 Senate budget resolution, would have extended a one-year prohibition of Medicaid funding for abortion providers like Planned Parenthood. In July 2025, abortion providers were precluded from receiving Medicaid dollars for one year due to the passage of the One Big Beautiful Bill. This amendment would have prevented abortion providers from regaining funding on July 4, 2026. Planned Parenthood, the nation’s largest abortion provider, also provides gender transition procedures, including for minors. Taxpayer funds should not be used to end human life or to inflict harm on individuals struggling with distress related to their sex.

The threshold for this Motion was set at 60 votes.

(Failed on 4/23/2026, 50 yeas to 48 nays, Roll Call No. 97)

FRC Action supported this Motion.
