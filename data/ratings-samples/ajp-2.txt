Our methodology includes every item that
Members had an opportunity to act on, and

in which we scored only post-October 7 items and rolled them into a weighted percentage
score based on votes, Dear Colleague letters,
public statements, and cosponsorships.
House Members were judged mostly on votes
and letters, while Senators were judged more
heavily on statements, because there were
fewer legislative opportunities in the Senate.
