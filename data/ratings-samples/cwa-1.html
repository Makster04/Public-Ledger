CWA Congressional Scorecard

Holding Congress Accountable

CWA’s Congressional Scorecard tracks how members of Congress vote on issues that affect workers. View the scorecard here:

The scorecard evaluates lawmakers based on their support for policies that protect workers’ rights, strengthen unions, and improve economic security for working families.

By bringing this information together in one place, CWA helps members understand where elected officials stand on issues important to working people.
