How We Calculate Immigration Grades

Our team analyzes every immigration bill and updates grades weekly based on your Congress Members' votes, bill sponsorships, and cosponsorships. Bills are graded on how they reduce illegal immigration and lower legal immigration levels.

The overall grade is a weighted average across all categories — with Great Solutions counting double.

**The weighting described above applies to the current Congress. Grades from previous Congresses may use slightly different category weights.
