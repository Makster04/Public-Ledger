AAUW Action Fund Congressional Voting Record

How Do Your U.S. Senators and U.S. Representatives Vote?

The AAUW Action Fund Congressional Voting Record provides information about elected federal legislators through the votes they cast and the bills they co-sponsor on issues that are critical to the Action Fund’s mission.

The Congressional Voting Record presents senators’ and representatives’ records on AAUW priorities, which were decided on the basis of the AAUW Public Policy Principles & Priorities, adopted by AAUW members. These priorities range from education, to economic security, to civil rights.

Find out where your elected officials stand!

Download the Congressional Voting Record for the 119th Congress.

Using the Congressional Voting Record

AAUW members and supporters have a long history of lobbying Congress and holding legislators accountable for how they vote. Use the Congressional Voting Record to advocate for AAUW’s priority policy issues by:

  • Referring to information in the voting record during community issue forums or at town hall meetings held by your members of Congress.
  • Writing letters to the editor (LTEs), blog posts, or op-eds about a legislator’s position on AAUW’s priority policy issues.
  • Distributing copies during local voter education events.
  • Sharing the guide with coalition partners, friends, family, and prospective AAUW members. Don’t forget to tag your posts on Facebook, Instagram, and other social media forums using #ItsMyVote.
