Our Mission

Fixing our democracy by getting big money out of politics and protecting the right to vote. We're working to end our rigged political system by electing reform champions, passing meaningful legislative reforms, and elevating these issues in the national conversation.

For 11 years, End Citizens United has been a leading voice for meaningful campaign finance reform. We're proud to work in partnership with real democracy champions to overturn Citizens United, end unlimited and undisclosed money in politics, and protect and expand the right to vote.

We're building a movement to take on corruption and Big Money in politics.
