2026 Voter Guide

Congressional Voter Guide

Candidates who have established a voting record, either in the office for which they are running or in another elected office, are evaluated primarily on the basis of that voting record. However, sponsorship of pro or anti-gun legislation and other such factors can affect their grade. These incumbent candidates are marked with an asterisk (*).

If an incumbent or challenger has not established a voting record or demonstrated his or her position in some other way, that candidate is evaluated on the basis of his or her responses to the GOA Federal Candidate Survey or public statements.

Every candidate, whether an incumbent or challenger, begins with an “A” and is then down-graded on the basis of each anti-gun position or vote.
