House - 119th 2nd Session Vote Descriptions

1. Breaking the Gridlock Act

Sponsored by Rep. James McGovern (D-MA), the Breaking the Gridlock Act (H.R. 1834) would have resurrected the enhanced subsidies of the so-called Affordable Care Act (ACA) that expired at the end of 2025 for an additional three years without providing protections from taxpayer funding for abortions and gender transition procedures. ACA healthcare plans have included funding for these procedures from the start, and these enhanced subsidies further perpetuate the problem. Furthermore, these COVID-era subsidies were meant to be temporary, specifically for the duration of the global pandemic, which ended years ago. Abortion and gender transition procedures are not health care, and taxpayers should not be forced to fund procedures that end unborn human life and inflict lasting harm on individuals struggling with their identity.

(Passed on 1/8/2026, 230 yeas to 196 nays, Roll Call No. 11)

FRC Action opposed this Bill.

2. Supporting Pregnant and Parenting Women and Families Act

Sponsored by Rep. Michelle Fischbach (R-MN), the Supporting Pregnant and Parenting Women and Families Act (H.R. 6945) amended the Social Security Act to clarify the authority of states to use existing funds through the Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) program to support pregnancy resource centers. These centers provide practical support to mothers and fathers with new babies, including those facing unplanned pregnancies. Following a rule issued by the Biden-era U.S. Department of Health and Human Services that could have restricted TANF funding for these centers, protections like the ones contained in this bill are necessary to ensure that pregnancy resource centers remain eligible.

(Passed on 1/21/2026, 215 yeas to 209 nays, Roll Call No. 37)

FRC Action supported this Bill.

View the Final Vote Results

3. Pregnant Students' Rights Act

Sponsored by Rep. Ashley Hinson (R-IA), the Pregnant Students’ Rights Act (H.R. 6359) amended the Higher Education Act to require colleges and universities to inform female students about their rights and protections to carry their pregnancies to term. Under the bill, these women would be informed of available resources and accommodations, such as the opportunity to be granted modified class schedules and excused absences, programs available on campus, and organizations in the local community offering support to young mothers. This bill would help ensure that young women are adequately informed of their options to choose life and continue their education.

(Passed on 1/22/2026, 217 yeas to 211 nays, Roll Call No. 47)

FRC Action supported this Bill.

View the Final Vote Results

4. Stopping Indoctrination and Protecting Kids Act

Sponsored by Rep. Tim Walberg (R-MI), the Stopping Indoctrination and Protecting Kids Act (H.R. 2616) would establish a requirement, as a condition of receiving federal funds, that any elementary or middle school must obtain parental consent before making any changes to a child’s pronouns, sex marker, or preferred name on school forms. Schools must also obtain parental permission before changing a child’s sex-based accommodations, such as locker rooms and bathrooms. Parents have a right to know if public schools are influencing their children to believe that they were “born in the wrong body.”

(Passed on 5/20/2026, 217 yeas to 198 nays, Roll Call No. 184)

FRC Action supported this Bill.
