# Ideas, quickest to longest

"My part" is the coding; "your part" is research, review and decisions, which is often the slower half.

| Idea | Complexity | My part | Your part | Main hurdle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Donation links** | Low | About an hour | A few hours: find and check each candidate's official donation page | No API lists donation pages, so they're collected by hand. Every candidate needs one, so neither party looks favored. |
| **Donor hover details** | Medium | A few hours | The big part: a one-line summary and a cited stance for every donor organization, likely hundreds | The stance labels need sources to stay nonpartisan. |
| **Reactions and comments** | Medium (likes only) to High (comments) | Likes: about a day. Comments: several days, with spam protection and reporting. | **Ongoing moderation**, plus a privacy policy and comment rules | Political comment sections attract spam and brigading. Moderation never ends. |

**Donor hover details:** today the hover shows one sentence. It would show three bullet points:
- **Sector:** the field it works in (for example, Environment, Unions, Social Justice, Business or Health Care).
- **What they do:** a one-line summary of the organization's main work.
- **Political stance:** whether it's generally considered liberal, conservative or nonpartisan, based on a cited source such as the group's own scorecards or the candidates it funds.

**Costs money:** only the AI assistant (Galileo, below) would have ongoing costs. Everything else stays free.

Ideas from comparing the site with [integrityindex.us](https://integrityindex.us), about how people find and share it.

# Proposed indices

Not calculated anywhere on the site. Each would need a documented formula, evidence minimums, source-backed code, tests and your decision before it's added.

- **Flip-Flop Index 🩴** and **Pants on Fire Index 🔥** (needs `GOOGLE_API_KEY`): planned additions to Integrity's Honesty component.
- **Speech Donor Alignment:** of their speeches on an issue a top donor lobbied on, the share that took the donor's side. Shown as "planned" on the Money & Influence tab.
- **Get Things Done 🏗️**, a possible fifth grade, only if you decide to add it: Get It Done ✅ (bills advanced), No-Show 🪑 (missed votes; needs every roll call in the current Congress), Across the Aisle 🌉 (cross-party sponsorship) and Party Rebel 🤠 (from Voteview, beside the grade).
- **Worker Backed ✊** and **Grassroots 🌱** (the FEC gift-size data it needs is now refreshed): considered for People First.
- **Super PAC Dependence 💸:** super PAC spending for the candidate ÷ (campaign funds raised + all outside spending for the candidate), over the same dates.
- **Lobbyist Overlap 🏛️**, **Contracts for Donors 📝** (needs a table matching agencies to the committees that oversee them) and **Foreign Agent Flag 🚩**.
- **Interest group ratings card:** Heritage Action, AFL-CIO, LCV, ACLU and HRC ratings, imported by hand once a year, always shown in opposing pairs and never counted in a grade.

# Major Roles needed
- **Coverage leads:** People assigned to specific politicians, parts of the project or data sources (API keys).
- **NLP specialists:** People with natural language processing experience. They'll become more important as we analyze speeches, videos and long quotes, and connect what politicians say to their donor groups.

# Feature Ideas
- **Simpler layout:** reorganize the information, tabs and charts so they're easy to read for a non-technical audience, with less small text on each page.
- **Election forecast map:** our own map predicting who wins each race, with its own published methodology for how predictions are calculated.
- **AI assistant (working name: Galileo):** an AI agent that answers visitors' questions about candidates and bills.
