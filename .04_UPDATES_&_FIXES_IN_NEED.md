### Refresh speed

Records arrive too slowly. Today the local server reads at most 40 pages a minute (the hosted version 8 every five minutes); some sources are capped very low (SAM.gov 1, GDELT 2, Wayback and CourtListener 3, the New York Times 4); about 1,500 checks run once a day; sources without keys never run; and updates stop when your Mac's server stops.

Options: raise `LOCAL_STEPS` (up to 200), raise a source's cap where its published limit allows, check the most important people and sources first, move rarely changing sources to weekly, add the missing keys, and deploy to Cloudflare so updates run when your Mac is off.

**Open question:** which records matter most to have fast, and which can wait a day or a week.
