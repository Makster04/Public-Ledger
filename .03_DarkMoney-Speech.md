# How Public Ledger connects dark money to speeches

What dark money is, and what the site can and can't show about it: see [.03_DarkMoney-Politicians.md](.03_DarkMoney-Politicians.md).

The connection between money and what politicians say isn't measured yet. On the Money & Influence tab, the Speech Donor Alignment box says "Not measured yet". This file describes what's collected and how the connection will work.

## What's collected

- **Floor speeches** from the Congressional Record, read daily and matched to members.
- **Press releases** from members' official websites.
- **Campaign website text, candidate questionnaires and posts.** Threads posts need a token that isn't set yet.
- No audio or video.

## How the money connection will work (planned)

- **The group's side on an issue:** taken from the votes it took a side on, which are already linked to the same issue questions the site uses for Ideology and Your match.
- **The politician's side:** taken from their statements tagged to that issue.
- **Compared with their party:** how often their statements match their funders' sides, beside how often their party's statements do on the same issues.
- **Before or after the money:** whether they said it before the group first gave or spent for them. A position held before the support isn't a change.
- **Position changes:** statements on the same issue that switch sides, with both quotes and dates.
- **No guessing:** "Position unclear" when a statement takes no clear side, and "No documented position" when the group has none. Mentioning an issue never counts as taking a side.

## What's needed

More floor speeches sorted by issue and side, either with a free model running locally plus review, or by hand. Not with a paid service.

## What it won't show

That money changed what anyone said, or who funded a group that hides its donors.
