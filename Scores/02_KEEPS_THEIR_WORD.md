# 2. Keeps Their Word

**Purpose:** of the promises they made to voters, the share they kept, judged by what they did afterward. It measures follow-through, not whether you agree with the promise.

**What it uses:**

- Promises from campaign sites, speeches, posts, press releases, debates and signed pledges ("I will…", "I'll vote to…").
- Each promise is matched to the votes and bills that decide it, using only actions dated after the promise.

**What the tab shows:** each promise with its verdict, the actions behind it, and the reason.

**How it's calculated:**

```text
100% × (Kept + ½ × Partially kept) ÷ (Kept + Partially kept + Broken + Allegedly broken)
```

- **Kept:** they voted for, sponsored or cosponsored it, and nothing afterward went against it.
- **Partially kept (½):** they backed a version that goes only partway.
- **Broken:** they voted against it.
- **Allegedly broken:** 6 months or more in office with no action on it while colleagues were acting on it. Counted as broken.
- **Not counted:** unclear cases (until reviewed), no action yet, and not tested yet (for example, a candidate who isn't in office).
- **For a president:** PolitiFact's tracker also counts.
- **Minimum:** 3 decided promises.
