# How the six scores work

Five are grades, shown as a percentage to one decimal and a letter (A is 90.0% and up, B 80.0%, C 70.0%, D 60.0%, F below 60.0%); the letter comes from the one-decimal percentage. Ideology is a position, not a grade. Missing data never counts as zero: a score without enough evidence shows "Not graded".

The parts inside two grades that aren't shares of anything are numbers from 0 to 100 with one decimal and no % sign: Plays by the Rules' Trade Conflicts, Stock Disclosure Dodger and Legal Trouble (the points its cases take off), and People First's Corporation Compromised with its two parts. Every other part is a percentage.

**On the home page's cards** they're in two groups: **Dark Money Affiliated Scores** (Big Money Independence, Plays by the Rules and People First) and **Other Scores** (Ideology, Keeps Their Word and Shows Up). The card's third group, **Wealth** (net worth, stocks owned and stock trades this cycle), comes from their own disclosures and isn't a score.

The calculations are in `public/six-scores.js`; Lobbied Bills' name matching is in `public/lobbied.js`.
