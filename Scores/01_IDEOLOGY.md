# 1. Ideology (a position, not a grade)

**Purpose:** where they stand from liberal to conservative, overall and by topic. Leaning one way isn't treated as good or bad, so it gets a scale instead of a letter.

**What it uses:** their votes, sponsored and cosponsored bills, official acts, dated statements, interest-group ratings, and Voteview's DW-NOMINATE score.

**What the tab shows:**

- The overall lean on a scale from −100 to +100: very liberal, liberal, moderate, conservative or very conservative.
- A lean for each category of questions, with the evidence behind it.
- Group ratings, shown in opposing pairs.
- DW-NOMINATE.
- Your Match: you pick your own sides, and it shows how close they are to you.

**How it's calculated:**

1. **Each question gets a stance.** Their actions count toward a side:
   - **Votes and cosponsorships:** 1 point each.
   - **Sponsoring a bill or an official act:** 1.5.
   - **A group rating:** up to 3.
   - **A statement:** 3 when they have no record on the question, 1 when they do.
   - **Limits:** each bill counts once. A statement is cancelled by a later vote the other way.
   - **Stance** = (points for one side − points for the other) ÷ all points. It needs at least 3 points.
2. **Category lean** = the average of its questions' leans.
3. **Overall lean** = the average of the categories. It needs at least 3 categories.
4. **Two independent checks,** shown beside the lean but not averaged in:
   - The ratings lean: a conservative group's score counts as 2 × score − 100, a liberal group's as the reverse.
   - DW-NOMINATE × 100.
