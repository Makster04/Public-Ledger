# Promises Delivered Index 📦

**Status: the Promises Delivered Index is Integrity's Honesty component (30%).** It was a separate published grade until Nov. 2, 2026; those historical grades remain accessible in the official monthly history. It remains unchanged in the official monthly history and is no longer a separate current headline grade. The reviewed commitments, quotes, checks, and sources now appear under Integrity → Honesty. It measures reviewed commitments and stated positions against documented actions; it does not measure whether a politician is generally truthful in every setting.

```text
Promises Delivered = 100 × (kept + 0.5 × partly kept or changed)
                           / counted checks
```

| Reviewed check | Credit |
|---|---:|
| Supported/kept | 1 |
| Partly supported or changed position | 0.5 |
| Contradicted | 0 |

At least three counted checks are required. Pending, ambiguous, vague, and outcome-dependent checks are excluded. A promise the politician could have acted on but did not by the end of the relevant term is recorded as contradicted. Counts and individual reviews remain visible on the profile with quotes, dates, and sources.

## President's tracker data

The president uses reviewed PolitiFact tracker tallies. Promise Kept earns 1; Compromise earns 0.5; Promise Broken earns 0. Stalled and In the Works are not counted until given a final rating. The same three-counted-check minimum applies.

## Money-related pledges

Pledge records are checked against FEC and legislative records as supporting evidence on the existing reviewed promise. The records check is shown alongside the verdict and can flag a conflict for human review; it does not silently replace the reviewed verdict or add a second Promise point. In the Integrity Fundraising component, four reviewed pledge categories separately contribute at most once each: kept +1, broken −1, qualifying cosponsorship with no pledge +1, otherwise 0. Pending pledges cannot receive the no-pledge fallback.

## Planned work not currently included

Flip-Flop and Pants on Fire are not implemented. They do not affect this grade or the Integrity Honesty component. Do not use the older proposed combined Promise formula until those methods are implemented, reviewed, and explicitly migrated.

See [Integrity](08_INTEGRITY_SCORE.md), [the canonical methodology](00_POLITICAL_SCORECARD_METHODOLOGY.md), and the [statement–action consistency pipeline](../docs/STATEMENT_ACTION_PIPELINE.md). Consistency analysis is separately calculated per statement and is not the Promises Delivered formula.
