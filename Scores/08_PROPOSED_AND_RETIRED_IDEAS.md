# Proposed and retired score ideas

This file records old design ideas so they are not mistaken for current project outputs. The following are **not implemented as current grades or indices**: Get It Done, No-Show, Across the Aisle, Party Rebel, Worker Backed, Grassroots, Super PAC Dependence, Lobbyist Overlap, Contracts for Donors, and Foreign Agent Flag. Do not report their older proposed formulas as active calculations.

The current published grades and indices are listed in the [canonical methodology](00_POLITICAL_SCORECARD_METHODOLOGY.md). Corporation Compromised Index and Donor Loyalty are implemented and described in [People First](04_PEOPLE_FIRST_SCORE.md) and [Money & Influence](07_MONEY_&_INFLUENCE_ANALYSIS.md).

## Historical context

Prior drafts proposed a fifth “Get Things Done” grade from bill advancement, attendance, and cross-party sponsorship. That grade was never adopted. The draft Grassroots, Super PAC Dependence, Lobbyist Overlap, contract, and foreign-agent measures likewise did not become grades or indices. Their appearance in older changelogs or planning notes is not evidence that they are calculated today.

## Future methodology changes

Any new grade or index needs a documented formula, evidence coverage and minimums, source-backed implementation, tests, and an explicit release decision. It must be listed in the score inventory and labeled as a proposal until it is actually present in the application.
